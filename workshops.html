A comprehensive guide to the Education, Health and Care Plan process from start to finish. Learn what to expect, what your rights are and how to engage effectively.

  • ✓What an EHCP is and who it is for
  • ✓How to request an EHC needs assessment

Feel confident and prepared before any SEND meeting or annual review. Understand your rights and how to advocate effectively for your child.
