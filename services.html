Our Services

Specialist SEND advocacy, EHCP guidance and parent support for families of neurodivergent children aged 0–25.

What We Offer

Specialist SEND Support

Every service NLH provides is designed to empower families and demystify the SEND system.

Service 01

SEND Advocacy & Guidance

NLH provides knowledgeable advocacy support to help families understand and exercise their rights within the SEND system. Whether you are just starting out or are already deep in a process, we help you navigate confidently.

We explain what the law says, what Local Authorities are required to do, and how to ensure your child's needs are properly recognised and met.

Service 02

EHCP Guidance & Applications

The EHCP (Education, Health and Care Plan) process can be complex and time-consuming. NLH provides step-by-step guidance through every stage — from deciding whether to request an assessment to the final plan being issued.

We help families understand what to include, how to gather evidence and how to make their voice heard throughout the process.

Enquire Now
Service 03

EHCP Amendments & Annual Reviews

Once an EHCP is in place, it must be kept up to date with your child's changing needs. NLH supports families through annual review preparation, requesting amendments and ensuring the plan reflects your child's current needs.

Enquire Now
✏️
👩‍🏫
Service 04

SEND Meeting Preparation

SEND meetings can feel daunting. NLH helps families prepare thoroughly — understanding what will be discussed, what questions to ask, what rights they have and how to ensure their child's needs are properly represented.

Going into a meeting prepared and informed makes a significant difference to outcomes for families.
