About NLH

Supporting Families Every Step of the Way

Neurodiversity Learning Hub (NLH) is a specialist service founded by Sidra Mirza to support families of neurodivergent children and young people aged 0–25. We believe every family deserves clear, accessible guidance when navigating complex SEND processes.

From EHCP applications to SEND meeting preparation, NLH provides knowledgeable, compassionate support so families feel empowered, informed and never alone.

Our Process

How It Works

Getting started with NLH is simple. We make it easy for families to access the support they need.

1

Submit Your Enquiry

Complete a short parent enquiry form so we can understand your family's situation and what support you need.

2

We Review & Respond

NLH reviews your enquiry and responds with information about how we can best support your family.

3

Tailored Support Begins

Guidance and advocacy is provided based on your child's individual needs and your family's circumstances.
