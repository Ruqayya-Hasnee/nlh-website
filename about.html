About Neurodiversity Learning Hub

Founded with a clear mission: to ensure no family navigates SEND processes alone.

Est. Nov 2025
Who We Are

Neurodiversity Learning Hub

Neurodiversity Learning Hub (NLH) is a specialist service dedicated to supporting families of neurodivergent children and young people aged 0–25 in navigating the often complex and overwhelming SEND system in England.

We understand that the world of SEND — with its acronyms, processes and systems — can feel intimidating. Our mission is to change that. NLH provides clear, compassionate and expert guidance so that every family feels informed, supported and empowered.

Since launching in November 2025, NLH has already supported over 25 families across Greater Manchester with EHCP applications, SEND meeting preparation, advocacy and guidance.

Our Founder

Sidra Mirza

Sidra Mirza founded Neurodiversity Learning Hub out of a deep commitment to ensuring families of neurodivergent children receive the support and guidance they deserve.

With specialist knowledge of SEND law, EHCP processes and Local Authority systems, Sidra brings both professional expertise and genuine compassion to every family she works with. Her approach is always clear, accessible and family-centred.

Sidra is passionate about demystifying the SEND system and helping parents feel confident advocates for their children — because every child deserves the right support in place.
