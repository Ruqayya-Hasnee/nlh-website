Why Donate

Your Support Makes a Difference

Supporting Neurodiversity Learning Hub helps us continue providing guidance and advocacy for families navigating SEND pathways.

Donations contribute towards developing resources, workshops and support for parents of neurodivergent children across Greater Manchester and beyond.

📚

Resources & Guides

Developing free, accessible resources for families navigating the SEND system.

🎓

Parent Workshops

Funding workshops that empower parents to understand and navigate SEND processes.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Support

Enabling NLH to support more families who are navigating complex SEND pathways.

🌐

Outreach & Awareness

Raising awareness of neurodiversity and the SEND system to reach more families.
